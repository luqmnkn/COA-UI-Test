Shariah Compliant · 100% Delivery

Our 100% Zakat Policy

Every single cent of your Zakat reaches eligible people in need. Not a penny is deducted for administration, fundraising, or marketing fees.

0% Admin Deductions

100% of your Zakat goes directly into the hands of eligible recipients (the poor, destitute, widows, and orphans).

Segregated Accounts

All Zakat funds are deposited into strictly restricted bank accounts and never co-mingled with administrative or general operational budgets.

Scholarly Oversight

Guided and verified by our Chairperson Mufti Mohammad Farhan, PhD, and independent certified auditors to verify strict Quranic compliance.

Direct Field Delivery

Where Your Zakat Goes

100% of your Zakat funds eligible individuals: widows, orphans, disabled children, and families living in extreme poverty.

Widows & Impoverished Families

Monthly food hampers, clean water, and emergency medical kits directly handed to verified eligible families.

Orphan & Child Education

Tuition, books, uniforms, and daily hot school meals for 570+ out-of-school students at Takmeel schools.

Gaza: Medical & Mobility Aid

Prosthetics, physical rehabilitation, wheelchairs, and trauma care for individuals wounded in Gaza.

Free Psychiatric Medical Care

Providing free clinical checkups, psychiatric medications, and therapeutic support for destitute patients.

Complete Transparency

How Are Our Operations Funded?

People often ask: If 0% is taken from Zakat, how does Children of Adam keep the lights on?

All operational, logistics, software, and administrative overheads are funded 100% separately through dedicated private gifts from our trustees, board members, and designated non-Zakat administrative sponsorships.
