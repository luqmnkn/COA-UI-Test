Our Purpose & Story

Give a helping hand to those who need it!

Children of Adam (COA) began in 2011, when a group of like-minded professionals on Long Island, New York, decided to turn concern into action. Today we raise empathetic young people guided by Islamic knowledge, and we help build sustainable futures, especially in under-resourced communities facing poverty, disease and illiteracy.

Like our tree logo, we grow from strong roots: the scholars, teachers, parents and donors who nurture us.
