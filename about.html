About Children of Adam

"Our name reminds us of what we share: every one of us is a child of Adam."

Who We Are

Rooted in Concern, Built for Action.

Children of Adam (COA) is a US-based 501(c)(3) non-profit founded in 2011 by like-minded professionals on Long Island, New York. We exist to raise empathetic, compassionate young people who are guided by Islamic knowledge and motivated to improve the world.

We help people build sustainable futures, especially in under-resourced rural communities facing poverty, disease and illiteracy. At home, we run learning, welfare and wellness programs for our neighbors. Abroad, we build water plants, schools, women's wellness centers and a hospital, and we respond fast when disaster strikes.

Global Standards

Our Impact on the UN SDGs

Our humanitarian and educational work directly aligns with 7 United Nations Sustainable Development Goals:

SDG 1: No Poverty

Care trucks, clothing donation bins, Cars 4 Jannah vehicle donations, and emergency cash assistance.

SDG 2: Zero Hunger

Soup kitchen, food bank, Saturday mobile community breakfast, and widow ration packs.

SDG 3: Good Health & Wellbeing

Free psychiatric clinics, Bani Adam Hospital, healing circles, and Mirpur women's wellness centers.

SDG 4: Quality Education

Deen 4 Teens, math tutoring, and 6 Takmeel informal schools educating 570+ out-of-school children.

SDG 6: Clean Water & Sanitation

Qatraa: 11 filtration plants in Islamabad tested regularly, serving 1,000+ residents every single day.

SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities

Support for Muslim refugee families in New York, resettlement aid, housing support, and social inclusion.

SDG 11: Sustainable Cities

Center of Excellence on Long Island: Youth Leadership Hub, Family Unity Center, and community hub.
